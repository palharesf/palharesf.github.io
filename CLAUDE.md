# CLAUDE.md — palharesf.github.io

## Project Overview

Fernando Palhares' personal developer portfolio. A pure static site (HTML/CSS/vanilla JS — no frameworks, no build tools) showcasing projects in Python and JavaScript: browser games, automation scripts, userscripts, and web applications.

---

## Design Context

### Users

Primary audience is **recruiters and hiring managers** — people who spend seconds deciding whether to keep reading. They need to quickly assess skills, see proof of work, and form a confident first impression. Technical depth matters, but clarity and polish are what keeps them on the page.

### Brand Personality

**Professional, calm, trustworthy.**

The site represents a developer who ships quality work and takes craft seriously. No gimmicks, no noise — just well-considered decisions that signal competence. The personality is measured and confident, not flashy.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Dark theme** (navy + teal) — existing direction, keep it. It reads as technical and considered, not edgy.
- **Accent color** (`#64ffda` cyan-green) — use sparingly as a signal of precision and attention. Overuse dilutes its impact.
- **Typography-led** — generous whitespace, strong hierarchy, nothing fighting for attention.
- **Micro-interactions** — subtle hover effects and transitions communicate craft without demanding attention.
- **No strong external reference** — the current aesthetic is the right direction; refinement over reinvention.

Anti-references: avoid anything that feels loud, gimmicky, over-animated, or "trying too hard." The opposite of trustworthy is chaotic.

### Design Principles

1. **Craft over decoration** — every visual decision should signal quality. Restraint is a feature. If an element doesn't earn its place, remove it.
2. **Clarity at first glance** — recruiters spend seconds. Information hierarchy must be immediately obvious: who, what, why hire them.
3. **Purposeful motion** — animations exist to guide attention and confirm interactions, not to impress. Subtle and fast beats slow and showy.
4. **Consistent precision** — spacing, alignment, and sizing should be visibly intentional. Inconsistency undermines trust.
5. **Content is the hero** — the work speaks for itself. The UI is a frame, not the painting.
