A community tool for hiding giveaway secrets behind a classic memory card game. Creators configure board size (4–18 pairs), board mode (random or fixed layout), and optional mistake and time limits. About Me

My path into programming started with curiosity — wanting to understand how things worked, then build my own versions. That same drive still shapes how I approach problems today.

I believe in the power of simple, well-crafted code. While modern development often pushes us toward complex frameworks and heavy dependencies, I find satisfaction in building lean, performant applications that do exactly what they need to do.
