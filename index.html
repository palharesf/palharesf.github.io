About Me

I'm Fernando Palhares, a developer with a passion for building practical, efficient tools that solve real problems. My journey into programming started with curiosity about how things work under the hood, and that curiosity continues to drive my learning and development today.

I believe in the power of simple, well-crafted code. While modern development often pushes us toward complex frameworks and heavy dependencies, I find satisfaction in building lean, performant applications that do exactly what they need to do.

My primary languages are Python and JavaScript, which together cover a remarkable range of use cases. I'm also exploring ways to make my projects more accessible and educational for other developers through detailed documentation and clean, commented source code.
