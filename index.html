Steam Deck Plugin · Public Beta

HLTB Sync for Deck

A Decky plugin that tracks gaming sessions and updates your HowLongToBeat account when you close a game. Runs entirely on the Steam Deck, excludes suspended time, and saves sessions offline. Tested on real Steam Deck hardware and released as a public beta. Existing notes, ratings, and completion status stay intact.

About Me

I build software around problems I encounter: repetitive browser tasks, community games, and keeping playtime in sync across tools. My projects span web interfaces, automation, and integrations.

I care about what happens beyond the happy path. In HLTB Sync, that means preserving sessions offline, excluding suspension, and checking uncertain submissions before sending again. I publish source code, document limitations, and test on the hardware people use.
